**Preface**

I didn't have much to write about for this season, but there were a couple of fun positions I have to talk about. This was probably the season I actually went the deepest into the timebank for because I had some real life obligations to take care of at the same time. Would those get in the way of trying to play well?
<br><br>
**Section 1: Always On the Wrong End**
I jumped out to an early lead against Blibble after my 104 point bingo of ZABeTAS# on my previous turn, but Blibble hit right back with UNTIDI(e)R to stay in 1 bingo range. 

![](/images/uploads/20261118-223457.png)

Here, I made a small error with 3I WAG for 33, going up by 94. Slightly better would have been to play 1H CWM for 36, scoring 3 more and keeping a more balanced leave with some nice floaters available for 8 letter bingoes. 

Unfortunately, Blibble then slams down the highest scoring play that anyone has ever played on me in now 2834 rated games played on Woogles, RO(U)GHENS across two triple word scores for a devastating 194 points, leaving me down 100.

![](/images/uploads/20261118-223740.png)

My bingo tiles never quite come together for the rest of a game and I made a miss of CESTODE, and I wound up losing by 84.

<br><br>
Up 4 points against KoalaJ, can you spot the best move here?

![](/images/uploads/20261118-223921.png)

After a while of searching, I couldn't find anything great and settled on CALI(G)oES# to neutralize the open G. This was not the right play at all. Somehow, I completely missed the common word LE(G)ACIeS, which plays for 167 points spanning both triples: an 84 point error. I have a pretty tough time anagramming with the blank and this was a pretty bad time to make a miss with the blank. I did draw another bingo immediately afterwards to put the game out of reach, but I could've sealed the game a turn earlier.
<br><br>

Finally, early in this game against shafiquet when I'm trailing by 60 after their bingo using both blanks, I made an even larger error than LE(G)ACIeS.
![](/images/uploads/20261118-224234.png)

I played 14B ME(T)IC# for 30, blocking the triple and hoping to draw something good. First of all, I didn't remember E(METIC) was an available hook, which offers extremely easy scoring plays to shafiquet. Worse, I managed to miss a second triple-triple this season. 

That triple-triple is MEI(O)CYTE#, scoring 185, a 155 point and 63.8% win percentage error. I didn't know this word but this really highlights that I need to continue to work on studying, though it's pretty difficult currently because I'm busy with other work. 

Once again, I was not punished for my error and got lucky to escape with the win in this game after landing a 70 point J play in the preendgame.
<br><br>
**Section 2: The Obligatory Preendgame**
It's not quite a Xadreco recap article if I don't have something to say about a preendgame :)
![](/images/uploads/20261118-225054.png)

I'm trailing by 23 points here vs JcrON with 3 tiles left in the bag. (Unseen: ACFILNNTUZ) I have some easy bingoes available here such as 10A ABlEIST or bABIEST, hooking the S onto GUV, for 76 or 72 points respectively. These put me up about 50 points and initially look quite solid. However, there are some things I need to worry about if I play these moves. With the more defensive bABIEST, Macondo suggests JcrON has a counter bingo that immediately wins the game about 7.3% of the time. Additionally, with only 3 vowels compared to 7 consonants unseen, I could be in some trouble if I draw 3 consonants, especially if I draw the unseen C, which is quite unwieldy on this closed board. JcrON could then block the open vowels in bABIEST, and likely beat me by playing out in 2 with the Z unseen to give JcrON some extra scoring punch. 

With immediate bingoes looking a bit risky, I formed some alternative plans. I probably don't want to empty the bag here by playing 3 or more tiles, because that will give JcrON perfect information that can help win a tight endgame or block my bingoes. One option to avoid emptying the bag is 11D AB for 18.

![](/images/uploads/20261118-230418.png)

 This sets up a new lane, hooking my S or T onto AB for 7 letter bingoes. This is a pretty decent play, but there are a couple situations where I might be in trouble. There are a few 7 letter bingoes still unseen such as LUNATIC, and depending on what I draw, I might not have an overlapping bingo that wins me the game, such as if the N is the final tile in the bag, I have nothing playable with EFISTZ?. These scenarios are not particularly likely, but they aren't great. The game could also occasionally be close if JcrON plays a high scoring Z play above the AT formed by AB such as ZIN. These plays close the lane and put JcrON ahead by 40, and if I don't have a bingo through the Z, it's not always the easiest to make up those deficits, even though I have the S to hook (OHM)S in the top right corner.

There's another place to play the same tiles, however, with AB(OHM) for only 15.

![](/images/uploads/20261118-230734.png)

This opens lanes that are much harder to block. It looks like this might give back some devastating Z plays to the triple, but there aren't any available in this situation. Many plays hitting the triple will leave easy S hooks or good tiles in space for me to bingo to, and I still have the T in TOEA available, which has good synergy with the EIS left on my rack. There are still bingoes for JcrON here, but they all have problems. One such bingo is the stunning 4E INFLAT(IO)N with 3 overlaps for 80. Although this would put JrcON ahead by 88, this leaves an easy S hook above their play forming S(AL). If JcrON has this bingo, then I actually still win if the Z is in the bag, after I bingo out and catch 20 points for it. Other high scoring bingoes like 2B ANNICUT# for 82 open the same easy -S bingo lanes. In fact, I actually still win 100% of the time if JcrON specifically has ANNICUT# because I barely score enough with Z bingoes hitting the double word score, or catch 20 for the Z once again. 

In the actual game, the final tiles in the bag were FLU. After playing AB(OHM), I drew FL, giving me the rack EFILST?. At that point, I was pretty confident I was going to win because I have bingoes above and below the A in ABOHM, as well as bingoes to the T in TOEA, so JrcON cannot block all of them. 

From JrcON's perspective, the final tiles in the bag are SULFITE + ?, so there is no way to win even if the blank is in the bag, because I can bingo with SULFITE both above and below ABOHM, and I have FLUTIES(T) to the T in TOEA. JcrON made their top preendgame play of CAZ# for 52 to score well and offload their highest scoring tiles, and I bingoed out with 4C FeLSITE for 77 +10 for unplayed tiles to win by 27.

I didn't draw many blanks this season, but I certainly got lucky in other ways. It was a pretty good season for me for playing good endgames after having a couple recent seasons of endgame points lost in the 200 point range.
