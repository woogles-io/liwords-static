**Game 1: ADAPT**

<p style="color: crimson;">I’m up 4 points with a good rack after Inverse’s bingo, but I make a pretty good size error here.</p>

![](/images/uploads/20261026-222550.png)

<p style="color: crimson;">I spotted one of my available bingoes of AIRINGS for 78 hooking (NAVARIN)S pretty much immediately, but was worried about what the play opened, AIRINGS slots the A 1 square in front of the double letter square to the middle right triple. Because I was worried about this, I made a big overadjustment and played 10G (P)RAISING for 11 less. This play actually offers more average points per turn to Inverse than AIRINGS despite not opening the double letter - triple word combo. (P)RAISING floats the helpful I, N, and G for 8 letter bingoes, as well as the dangerous S. <p style="color: crimson;">Besides AIRINGS and (P)RAISING, I actually missed my best play of RAGINIS in the same spot hooking (NAVARIN)S. ![](/images/uploads/20261026-222803.png)

<p style="color: crimson;">On his last turn, Inverse punished my poor play of (P)RAISING with a big bingo, even using all 1-pointers of TOASTIE# for 86, cutting my lead to 18. I don’t need to go all in on drawing another bingo to try and cement the win as long as I keep scoring reasonably. Given the unseen pool containing both the Q and the Z, I also need to be worried that if I sacrifice points to keep a better leave, then common 2-tile scoring plays to the left of TOASTIE# such as QI or ZE will take the lead and block my best line.</p>

<p style="color: crimson;">Instead of a standard play, I went with Macondo’s favorite play of L8 WR(I)TE for 24. This pushes my lead up to 42 points, rather than 32, and there are some good tiles I’d like to draw including the Z and second blank. </p>

![](/images/uploads/20261026-222850.png)

<p style="color: crimson;">I’ve now drawn the blank after my previous play of WR(I)TE, but also the H and the V. <p style="color: crimson;">Feeling DISOWN(ED) was too unlikely to win in the endgame, I felt the need to form some alternative plans. I decided that a bingo was the best way to try and steal the game, and as such, I made the play of 10L (A)N for just 2 points.</p>
