**Preface**

Season 26 of biweekly leagues officially marks the last season of year 1 of league. In that time, over 400 unique players have played at least 1 season of league, and of those, there are just 28 players who have played every season of league so far. Since then, I also wound up as the player who has played the most division 1 seasons without having won league once, at 20 seasons (not including season 26), despite having come close a couple of times. Was I finally able to break the curse?
<br><br>

**Game 1: Just a Bit Complacent**

With just 1 tile in the bag against trustmeimnotabot and holding a 53 point lead, the game is looking pretty much won.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-112504.png)

The unseen tile pool is AAFILNOT. The obvious play here that I quickly found and made is H1 JIV(E)S. This scores a very solid 45 points and sheds my highest scoring tiles, while also blocking A(B)LATION at the top of the board, if the F is in the bag. I didn't see any other reasonable spots for a bingo to fit, so I didn't give it much thought. 

I actually threw away 12.5% winning chances by not playing my only 100% winning play of C12 JISM for 35. The reason for this is that with the O in the bag, trustmeimnotabot is threatening a very sneaky play of C8 FANTAIL for 90 points, making 4 overlaps, to the left of AURA. After JIV(E)S, this bingo puts the score at 467-459, but then they catch me with GMNO, squeaking them over the finish line with a final score of 467-473. My best play of JISM blocks this bingo, while also scoring just enough to outrun A(B)LATION at the top.
<br><br>
**Game 2: Not 100%?**

It's jellomochas' turn here, already holding a 72 point lead, and with multiple playable bingoes. jellomochas quickly plays D4 NITROUS for 71, putting them up 143 points, which will easily outrun my bingoes through the open letters.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-113723.png)

However, there was a crazy possibility. With the unseen pool of AADENSTU, if the S is the final tile in the bag, I have an unbelievable winning play. With AADENTU on my rack, through that open C in CREDIT, I have the ridiculous ADUN(C)ATE as a triple-triple, making 5 overlaps for 167 points, easily winning the game. It is for this reason that Macondo suggests that the best plays are all strange looking moves such as C7 TRIUN(E) for just 13 points, denying the ability to overlap the G in GARRED.

In the actual game, on the previous turn, I already had the S, so there was no chance of drawing this game winning play. The T was the final tile in the bag, so I did have a nice option with E2 SAUNAED for 79 to bingo out, with 5 overlaps.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-114130.png)

<br><br>
**Game 3: Damn, That's a Big Punish**

Late in the game against JcrON, I'm down 108 after futzing around with non-bingo racks, trying to hit the (DAM)E hook with a bingo.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-114444.png)

I still have no bingoes available here, but I'm feeling like I'm in some pretty dire straits, so I made the play of 4K (G)UQIN# for 32. The unseen tile pool is pretty good, containing all the letters in RETINAS, as well as the blank, but I felt that I had to give myself a shot. Somehow, I managed to miss that DAM does not only take the E, P, and S hooks. Of course, it takes an N for DAMN, and with that hook, I have the massive N10 QUINE# for 83, slotting the Q on the triple letter score and doubling the whole word. I'm still not all that likely to win after this, being down 25 and a turn, but this gives me much better practical chances. 

As a well deserved karmic punishment for this, JcrON immediately slams down JAU(N)TiER across 2 triple word scores for 176 points to make the game a gigantic blowout, and cratering my spread tiebreaker somewhat. ![](/images/uploads/20261127-115204.png)

I did manage to bingo out with N6 TRAINER to get some spread back (missing the better N7 RETRAIN/TERRAIN, once again making DAMN, go figure). Had I spotted the DAMN hook, JcrON's top equity play is 9M JEU for 33, hooking E(QUINE), so my miss was functionally a 194 point error.
<br><br>
**Game 4: Getting that Luck In**

Midway through my game with barikayak, I've just lost my small lead that I've been maintaining after their bingo of Ba(N)DEROL.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-115828.png)

Their bingo also blocked the bingo I was hoping to play of LENT(O)IDS through the O, so this was a pretty big swing. I'm only down 22 here, so I'm by no means out of the game. Unfortunately, there aren't a lot of good scoring options here with my low point tiles, so I decided that I need to try to shoot for a bingo. I figured the L was the tile with the least synergy with the rest of my rack, so I looked for ways to fish it off. The highest scoring play using just my L is 7B L(AH) for 7. There are no bingoes to draw using the C at the top of the board, so this play hits bingoes with 10 of the unseen 23 tiles (AKOUV) through the I in MINI, so not great odds for a play scoring so little. 

DEINST hits a lot of 7 letter bingoes, yet none of them fit on the board as-is, so I looked at a lower scoring L fish of 9L (E)L for just 3 points instead. This opens a line for any 7 letter bingoes I draw starting with D, S, or T. Because of this new line, if both areas remain unobstructed, I can also draw bingoes if I draw the I, L, N, P, and T, so this hits 21/23 of the unseen tiles, only missing if I draw the J or Q.

In the game, I draw another T from the bag, so after barikayak's high scoring response of TAJ for 43, I played my top bingo of DISTENT for 75 to get back into the game (whereas if I had played L(AH), I wouldn't have had any playable bingoes). barikayak responded with a high scoring QUO(T)A for 45 through the T in DISTENT, so I was still feeling pretty bad about my chances at first.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-121330.png)

Luckily, I just barely spotted my common-word bingo of 1B (C)OATTAIL for 83 to take a 51 point lead, with just 1 tile in the bag. This play is not a guaranteed win, because if I draw the unseen V as the last tile, it will only play in 1 spot with 10J V(AR), which barikayak can block and then easily win by slowplaying the endgame. The final tile in the bag was actually the K, so I did end up winning pretty easily, but it definitely took me some luck to get there.

Interestingly, Macondo much prefers the play of 7B D(AH)L for 8 on the (E)L turn, but I'm not so sure why as it doesn't score much more, and it bingoes 30% less often.
<br><br>
**Game 5: The Q is the Worst**

Against andy, I'm down 62 points, but I do have a nice natural bingo available of BALANCE(S) for 64, but it opens a massive scoring/bingo spot with some strong tiles and both blanks unseen.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-122059.png)

andy plays 15C RANKER for 43 to retake the lead, sending us into the endgame, where we have some pretty interesting racks.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-122306.png)

I managed to pull both blanks on my replenishment pull, and andy is holding BQS. I immediately noticed that the Q is absolutely horrible on this board. There is nowhere to drop it by itself with words like QI or QAT, and the only spot that andy is threatening to play it is through the U in JUDOS with S(U)Q.

Of course, I first need to block this play to stick andy with the Q. I went with 9D (U)T for 2 points to save my highest scoring tiles for some other ideas. (This is not quite best). Acknowledging the potential for me to make setups, andy makes an astute choice to pass their turn, keeping the B and S to block my setups. After doing a bunch of math and testing different endgame sequences, I settled on the following sequence, which led to a 44 point win:

B10 (ZEA)s 12


1L (MO)BS 24


9C P(UT) 16


pass


1A XU 31


pass


J4 n(T)H 28 +Q


471-427

Despite not playing the endgame perfectly, I gained 85 points in the endgame, because being stuck with the Q is so bad.

Just to demonstrate how dangerous setups can be from this position, let's say andy played off their B and S early. If this happened, I might have plays such as 1L (MO)U, setting up plays like PreX hooking MOUP# for around 80 points.

<br><br>

**Game 6: Another Perfect Game!?** 

After having played my first perfect game ever against bnjy a couple seasons back, I absolutely didn't expect to get another one so quickly.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-130814.png)

On my very first turn against Inverse, I've got the 7 letter bingo of WAGERED on my rack, but because I can't overlap the C in COOEY, I'm forced to take it as a double-double for 48 through the E.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-130851.png)

I draw the Z, and after Inverse covers up the lower triple word score with FOLIA, although I have J4 REZ for 68 available, I have the even better 13F AZUR(I)TES for 106 to jump far into the lead. 

![](/images/uploads/20261127-131011.png)

A couple turns later, after having immediately drawn another bingo of OBT(A)INED, I have a bit of a clunky rack but can score well with my top move of O8 THRU(M) for 30, blocking both triple-triple lanes.

And it's a good thing I did, because Inverse plays 15A ENDITES for 94, meaning they had SEDI(M)ENT ready to go as a triple-triple through the M that I just blocked.

Fast forward to the pre-endgame, and with 7 in the bag, I'm actually trailing again by 12 points, after Inverse drew 2 more bingoes out of the bag after ENDITES, using both blanks.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-131306.png)

I've got way too many vowels here, but that might not be such a bad thing. The unseen tiles have just 4 vowels compared to 10 consonants, so extra vowels will help me absorb draws loaded with heavy consonants. My top scoring play here is B2 EAR(L)Y for 32, but it's not amazing. the AOU leave isn't so bad, but it's still a bit unbalanced. This also opens up the D(EARLY) and N(EARLY) hooks with both of those tiles unseen, though they aren't the easiest to use in this position. The biggest problem is what else EAR(L)Y opens up. There are a lot of easy scoring plays like KEF for 37 on the triple, and the open A will let Inverse dump a lot of consonants or the Q with Q(A)T. 

These are some reasons that I think that my actual play, 4D YEA for 26, is solidly better. 

![](/images/uploads/20261127-132436.png)

Holding the R keeps my rack a bit more balanced, but also it can give me occasional strong 6 letter plays to the bottom right triple hooking (FOLIA)R. This play also blocks all of the open vowels, so any extremely consonant heavy rack that Inverse might have is going to struggle a lot more on the resulting board, especially racks holding the Q, with the U now blocked. This also keeps the case A (though EAR(L)Y also does), so I'm the only one who can hook A(VOW) for a good score. Holding the U is certainly helpful as well, as I'll be the only one who can play the Q for a good score on this board, with multiple spots to play QUA for at least 30 points.

Inverse responds with 3A FECK for 36, emptying the bag and giving them a 22 point lead.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-132915.png)

Although this blocks my bingo I was hoping to play of QUORATE above the S in SPINNIER (and EQUATOR does not fit), my choice to keep the R gives me the gives me the ginormous 15J ROQUET 84, hooking the R to make FOLIAR, giving me too big of a lead.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-133111.png)

For what it's worth, Inverse also played quite well.

**Game 7: The Race for First**

My game with supernova was one of my last to finish, and it was an extremely important game. I had a ridiculously high spread at this point, so as long as I at least tied whoever was in second at the time in wins, then I would likely hold on to win the season. jellomochas was about tied at the time in their in-progress game with supernova. A win for jellomochas would put them at 10-4, and I was at 8-5 at the time, so I needed to win this game to be guaranteed the season win, but a loss could still hypothetically give me a win if jellomochas lost as well. 

![](/images/uploads/20261127-133807.png)

Early in the game, I've been working through some clunky racks that have stopped me from bingoing with the blank, but I get a nice stroke of luck right after supernova bingoes with the other blank, giving them a 49 point lead. I drew the massive D1 JARRINg for 102, jumping me back into the lead.

supernova draws another bingo right away with C7 ARABINS#, and I can only just about equalize the score after with my play of TAXING.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-134027.png)

I draw the Q immediately, but I have a good scoring play of VE(R)SED for 48, well worth leaving the Q, especially with 2 Us unseen. 

![](/images/uploads/20261127-134136.png)

A couple turns later, I'm still trying to draw some help for my Q, but I have another good play of O12 PEON for 41 points available, putting me up 63 and blocking the big (TAXING)S# hook, also going for one of the 3 unseen Is for 10J QI for 36 above FLOX#, while still holding a guaranteed B1 QAT for 27. 

![](/images/uploads/20261127-135043.png)

Moving to the preendgame, I'm up 39 with 6 tiles in the bag. I didn't think too long and went with E5 FY# for 35. I figured that going up 74 with such low scoring bingo lines available, I was pretty much guaranteed to win. 

![](/images/uploads/20261127-135510.png)

However, Macondo has a pretty interesting option of 10A TA(B)EFY# for just 24 that it prefers. 

![](/images/uploads/20261127-135527.png)

By playing 5 tiles, this ensures that if supernova bingoes immediately, they will only draw 1 tile from the bag, so they will not be able to score well with their outplay. This also blocks some bingoes through the B and D. Even freak high-scoring bingoes from the PE in the lower left corner actually still give me a good chance to win. For example, if supernova plays (PE)LERINES for 86, taking a 23 point lead, I actually still win 62.5% of the time, if the final tile in the bag was an I, O, R, or U. If any of these tiles are in the bag, I have the sneaky E2 (V)IBEY# for 48 to win the game. If instead, supernova plays the obscure (PE)RIBOLUS# for 92, taking an even bigger lead, I actually can still win 25% of the time, if an I is the final tile in the bag, because I have E2 (V)ENEY# for 42. This also ties if the R is in the bag, with the same play of (V)ENEY# available. Bingoes not from the PE (other than 9E LOUSIER, which still lets me win 7/8 because of plays from the V) score so little that I'll likely still win by hitting the triple or making any of my plays from the V.

In the real game, supernova did not have a playable bingo immediately, fishing with BY(E) for 19.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-140929.png)

Perhaps fittingly, I sealed the game, and by extension, the season win with a bingo from my favorite set, the 5 vowel bingoes, of 10E EATERIE# for 66, putting me up 121.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-141104.png)

supernova actually made an unfortunate typo of just (PE)RIL in the endgame, meaning to play (PE)RILOUS.

Perhaps even more fittingly, my last move was EN(D) to finish out this game.

![](/images/uploads/20261127-141219.png)

**Conclusion**

On the final season of year 1 of league, I finally managed to win my first season. I undeniably got lucky en route to winning league, as evidenced by my high 468.6 average game score. 

As a <s>Scrabble</s> OMGWords player, since season 1, my skill has changed a lot. In season 1, in terms of formal word study, I had not even completed the 3's, nor had I studied a single 8 letter bingo. I was only at 1000 7s learned at that time as well. Since then, although I've been on hiatus because of studies and general low motivation for a while, I'm at about 5000 each for 7s and 8s, and I'm slowly making my way through every 4. Since season 1, I attended my first ever in-person tournament and met and played with a bunch of awesome Scrabblers, some of which I'd actually played in league (or otherwise on Woogles) before. Hopefully when season 52 rolls around next year, I can say that I'm way deeper into the bingoes. 

Huge shoutout to Cesar and everyone else on the Woogles team, as well as all the people I've played with on Woogles for making everything possible.
